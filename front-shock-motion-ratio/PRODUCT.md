# Product

## Platform

Static web application generated by Python.

## Users

Bruin Formula Racing suspension and vehicle-dynamics engineers studying front shock packaging and motion ratio before committing to rocker or damper geometry.

## Product Purpose

Prescribe vertical wheel travel, solve the compatible double-wishbone and rocker pose, and inspect shock compression against wheel travel from the same geometry source.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Uses the configured six-member front suspension, rigid upright, and single-axis rocker.
- Positive wheel travel means bump/upward travel; the imported CAD basis retains −Z upward.
- Motion ratio is `d(vertical wheel travel) / d(shock compression)`.
- The chassis shock pickup and rocker pivot axis remain fixed.
- This is a kinematic packaging study. It does not include compliance, bump stops, tire deflection, damper side load, or structural stress.

## Product Principles

- One solved pose drives the 3D view, motion trace, and numerical readout.
- Show conventions and hard constraints directly in the working surface.
- Treat non-convergence or geometry limits as engineering findings, not hidden errors.
